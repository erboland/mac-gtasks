Privacy

Tasks for Mac is a native macOS app and desktop widget that syncs with Google Tasks. It does not run its own servers. This page is the policy linked from Google’s OAuth consent screen.

What the app asks Google for

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/tasks — read and update your task lists
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email — your account email, shown in the sidebar

Where data lives

OAuth tokens and a local copy of your lists stay on your Mac, in the App Group and Application Support folder for com.googletasks.Tasks. Completing a task in the widget or the app sends that change to Google’s Tasks API. Nothing is sent to the author of this app.

What it does not do

No analytics SDK. No ads. No account besides the Google account you sign in with.

Sign out

Use Sign Out in the sidebar to delete local tokens and restore sample tasks.
